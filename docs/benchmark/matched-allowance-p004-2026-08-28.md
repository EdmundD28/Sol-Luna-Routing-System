# Matched allowance campaign p004

Two counterbalanced pairs compared complete Sol-only delivery with one Sol High controller reusing one authentic Luna High worker across two rolling implementation packages. Route boundaries used the authenticated ChatGPT Usage dashboard in one unchanged five-hour window; outer review and reporting were excluded.

| Pair | Route | Five-hour points | Weekly points | Elapsed seconds | Independent acceptance |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 001 | Sol-only | 4 | 1 | 1416.249 | 29/32 |
| 001 | Sol-Luna | 1 | 0 | 1252.718 | 30/32 |
| 002 | Sol-Luna | 1 | 0 | 2578.013 | 30/32 |
| 002 | Sol-only | 3 | 1 | 2385.978 | 29/32 |

Displayed five-hour consumption totalled 7 points for Sol-only and 2 for Sol-Luna, a 3.5× point estimate. Weekly movement was 2 versus 0 points. With one-point endpoint uncertainty, the conservative five-hour advantage lower bound was only 0.5×. Sol-Luna total elapsed was 3830.731 seconds versus 3802.227 seconds for Sol-only, a 0.75% regression.

Every candidate failed the same frozen independent suite, chiefly because cross-executor duplicate and prefix-overlapping ownership paths were not rejected. Pair 001's orchestration mechanism itself passed: one Luna handled both packages, all Luna work was accepted, Sol did not shadow-implement it, reported checks were reused, and one evidence-backed repair was used. Pair 002 left a second package unrepaired after a different package consumed the campaign's single repair budget.

The campaign status is `HOLD`. It does not prove the 10× allowance target or equal quality and does not unlock additional active writers. It motivates three bounded production changes: optimize accepted Luna coverage rather than call count, reuse one Luna across adjacent stable-domain packages, and assign at most one positive-benefit focused repair per package rather than one for the whole task.
