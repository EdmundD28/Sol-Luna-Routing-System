# P107 Medium vault-migration calibration

P107 tested one coherent seven-module Markdown vault migration capability after
four rounds of specification review and independent review of 29 public plus 6
route-external checks. It was not a leaf portfolio or a repeat of the P106
module split. The account was not frozen and no allowance reading was taken.

One fresh Luna Medium owned every implementation path and was the sole public
suite executor. The first final suite failed eight tests. Existing output tied
them to link parsing/final-path mapping, case-folded vault collision detection,
and applied-path reporting. One focused same-Luna repair was allowed without
resending the task background.

The repair finished 29 tests with four failures and two platform skips. It still
mis-handled simultaneous move bodies, failed to rewrite links in a moved source
from its final parent, and did not reject a case-colliding vault. Hidden
acceptance was not run. Sol neither read nor rewrote the implementation. The
route boundary was 295.107 seconds, below the 15-minute measurement floor even
though quality failed.

Decision: reject quality and duration. The exact-byte, platform-sensitive link
and filesystem semantics made this a poor Medium route; the installed policy
already says such work normally needs High, while prior High evidence is
economically negative. Do not retry P107 by adding prompt detail or repair
rounds, and do not use it for an account-percentage experiment.
