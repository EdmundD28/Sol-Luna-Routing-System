# v0.38.0 evidence-bound High routing

Status: experimental effort-routing correction. It removes a contradictory High default; it does not establish general or included-plan percentage superiority.

## Problem

The Skill required the lowest reliable Luna effort and said High-or-above needed a failed lower-effort quality gate, but the adjacent sentence said strict serialization and adversarial work normally needed High. On P117 that label-based shortcut selected Luna High without a same-allocation Medium result.

## Change

Exact-byte, platform-sensitive, strict-serialization, and adversarial labels now raise the acceptance bar but never authorize High by themselves. Luna-owned critical-path work can use High or above only when matched task-family evidence shows the same allocation at lower effort missed a quality or defect gate. No field, tool, model round, or normal-path artifact is added.

## Evidence

The frozen P117 task, public suite, hidden referee, Sol High controller, one retained writer, ownership, single long wait, and stop conditions stayed fixed. Only Luna effort changed from High to Medium.

- Sol-only: 19/19 public, 10/10 hidden, `10.323980` credit equivalent, `241.744` seconds.
- Luna High long-wait route: 19/19 public, 10/10 hidden, `4.626947`, `300.868` seconds.
- Luna Medium long-wait route: 19/19 public, 10/10 hidden, `7.294941`, `160.060` seconds.

Medium therefore matched quality with zero formal repair or Sol rewrite, used `29.339838%` less credit equivalent than Sol-only, and finished `33.789463%` faster. It proved High was unnecessary on this task. The Medium writer itself cost only `0.328721`; one uncached Sol controller turn raised the route proxy and shows why a single credit sample remains noisy.

## Limits

- P117 is a development-set effort ablation, not an unseen second task family.
- The Medium writer ran the public full suite before and after its final `__all__` edit. Both passed, but this violated the single-final-suite protocol and its full cost remains included.
- The route did not clear the Skill's `50%` accepted-cost-reduction threshold; the measured reduction was `29.34%`.
- Official purchased-credit equivalent is a temporary relative proxy, not five-hour or weekly included-plan consumption.

The result justifies removing label-only High promotion. A frozen unseen task must still confirm the combined quality, credit, and time direction before any broad claim.
